Privacy Policy

Eat or Not: AI Food Check Support

For help, purchase issues, or privacy requests, contact Stepan Vakhtin at contact.stepan.vakhtin@mail.ru.

Reporting a problem

Please include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, the error message, and the approximate time of the issue with your time zone. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the app's purchase screen with the Apple account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple's subscription settings. For refunds, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

Scan history and privacy

You can remove scans or clear history in the app. Premium history synchronization requires iCloud. For information about photo analysis, storage, service providers, and deletion requests, read the Privacy Policy.

About results

AI results are general estimates and may be incorrect. They are not medical advice and must not be used to establish whether food is safe to eat or free of allergens.
